Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my streak?
Streaks reset automatically if you miss a day. Play every day to keep your streak alive.

How are points calculated?
Each game has its own scoring system. Generally, the earlier and faster you answer, the more points you earn.

When do new games become available?
New daily puzzles drop every day at midnight UTC.

How do I delete my account?
You can delete your account directly within the app. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This will permanently remove your account and all associated data. If you have any issues, email us at f.cansever67@gmail.com.

I found a bug. How do I report it?
Please email us at f.cansever67@gmail.com with a description of the issue and your device model.
